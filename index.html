Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our repair warranties, process, and hardware testing.

How long does screen replacement take?
Most smartphone screen replacements are completed in 30 to 45 minutes. If you walk into our shop or book an express appointment, our technicians immediately begin disassembling your device inside our cleanroom lab, and you can comfortably wait in our lounge with your phone restored the same hour.
Do you repair all smartphone brands?
Yes! We stock OEM-spec parts and calibration rigs for Apple iPhone, Samsung Galaxy (including foldables), Google Pixel, Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO, Oppo, Huawei, OnePlus, Motorola, and Sony Xperia. If you have an uncommon or specialty imported device, we can source genuine parts directly within 24 to 48 hours.
Do you offer repair warranties?
Absolutely. All screen and battery replacements include a 12-Month Comprehensive Warranty covering digitizer touch defects, display ghosting, frame separation, and internal component failure. Other micro-soldering and port services are covered under a minimum 6-month warranty. (Note: physical accidental drops or subsequent liquid immersion after repair are excluded).
How much does a screen replacement cost?
Cost depends on your device make, model, and display architecture (standard LCD, OLED, or curved Dynamic AMOLED 2X). Standard screen replacements typically range from $69 to $189, while high-end flagship curved panels range from $199 to $289. We always provide a 100% upfront, itemized, fixed quote with zero hidden labor charges.
Do I need an appointment?
Appointments are not required! Walk-ins are always welcomed at our repair center during business hours. However, scheduling online or giving us a quick call reserves your specific screen panel ahead of time and guarantees immediate priority bench service the moment you arrive.
Can you repair water-damaged phones?
Yes! We boast an 85%+ success rate with liquid-damaged smartphones. We immediately disassemble the unit, disconnect battery power, conduct multi-stage ultrasonic chemical cleaning to dissolve corrosion, and replace short-circuited capacitors or micro-traces. We strongly recommend bringing the device in as soon as possible without attempting to plug it into a charger.
